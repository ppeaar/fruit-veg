📝 How to Use

Method 1: Camera

  1. Click the "Start Camera" button
  2. Allow camera access when prompted
  3. Hold a fruit or vegetable in front of the camera
  4. Wait for the AI to identify the item
  5. View the prediction and confidence score

Method 2: Upload Image

  1. Click on the "Choose Image" button
  2. Select an image from your device
  3. The AI will automatically predict the fruit or vegetable
  4. View the prediction and confidence score
